## What was restored

The entire archived `legal_bot/data/` tree was restored from the previously released Final QA package, rather than fabricating or regenerating legal data. This includes the Iran legal corpus, supporting legal metadata/manifests, and the academic-source JSON asset.

The derived cache `.legal_research_stats_v140.pkl` was deliberately not copied. It is regenerated by the application from the source JSON and should not be treated as authoritative data.

Key restored dataset counts:

- `laws.json`: 173 law/package keys; 9,179 list records.
- `legal_glossary.json`: 400 entries.
- `important_terms.json`: 1,478 entries.
- `rulings.json`: 286 records (159 Supreme Court General Assembly rulings + 127 Administrative Justice Court rulings).
- `consultations.json`: 179 records.
- `samples_and_circulars.json`: 78 samples + 20 circulars.

These counts match the inventory recorded in `MACHINA_GROWTH_BATCH20_STAGE1_SEARCH_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-31.md` in the base package.

## Provenance and legal-status limitation

The restored files are byte-for-byte copies from the archived Final QA package. SHA-256 hashes for the principal files are recorded below. The accompanying `official_sources.json`, `law_catalog.json`, `quarantine_manifest.json`, and source manifests were restored alongside the corpus so that source and verification metadata are not discarded.

**Restoration is not legal verification.** The archived registry says its source inventory was last reviewed on 2026-08-20, and it explicitly distinguishes bundled/unverified, user-PDF-derived, source-backed, and quarantined records. A known URL or a record's presence in `laws.json` does not mean the text is currently in force or independently verified. No record was promoted to `verified`, no quarantine was removed, and no current-law claim was added. Before production legal reliance, the corpus still needs the agreed source-by-source validation against the project's authoritative-source policy (including the user's preference for Parliament sources).

## Compatibility correction

The first full-suite run after restoring data reached the tests and found three failures in `tests/test_stage5_ai.py`. Those test doubles still used the pre-preference-integration `ask_ai_question` signature and rejected the already-supported `preference_uid` keyword. The three mocks were updated to accept `preference_uid=None`; production application code was not changed for this correction.
